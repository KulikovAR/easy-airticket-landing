This page explains the cancellation and refund terms of the {{brand}} flight reservation service. It applies together with the {{link_distance_sales}} and the {{link_preinfo}}.

## 1. Cancellation before delivery

If you have paid but the reservation has not yet been created and the document has not been sent, you can cancel your order by writing to {{email}} or through our contact channels. In this case the full amount is refunded.

## 2. After delivery

Because the service is performed immediately with your consent, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised and no refund is given once the reservation document has been sent to your email (Distance Contracts Regulation, Art. 15(1)(ğ), (h)).

## 3. When the fee is refunded

- The reservation document cannot be delivered for reasons attributable to the Company,
- The document differs from the order due to a Company error and cannot be corrected,
- A duplicate payment was taken for the same order.

## 4. Free corrections and re-issue

- Spelling or date errors caused by the Company are corrected free of charge.
- If the airline cancels the flight or changes its schedule, a new reservation is created free of charge within the validity period.
- New reservations needed because of incorrect information from the customer or a change of dates are charged as a new order.

## 5. When the fee is not refunded

- The visa application is refused,
- The 7-day validity of the reservation expires,
- The customer changes or cancels their travel plans.

## 6. Refund process

Refund requests are reviewed within 3 business days. Approved refunds are made to the card used for payment within 14 days; the time for the amount to appear depends on your bank.
