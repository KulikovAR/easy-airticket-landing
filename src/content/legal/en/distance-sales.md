## Article 2 — Subject

This agreement sets out the rights and obligations of the parties, under the Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation, regarding the sale and performance of the flight reservation service for visa applications ordered electronically by the Consumer on {{website}} (the “Site”).

## Article 3 — Service and price

3.1. The Company creates a flight reservation in an airline reservation system based on the route, dates and passenger details chosen by the Consumer and delivers the reservation document containing the PNR code electronically.

3.2. The service fee is **{{price}}**, all taxes included. No ticket price is charged and no ticket is issued.

3.3. The reservation is valid for up to 7 days and is cancelled automatically at the end of that period. It cannot be used for travel, boarding or entry into a country.

## Article 4 — Obligations of the parties

4.1. The Consumer confirms that the name, passport and contact details given in the order are accurate and match the passport. The Company is not liable for consequences of incorrect information.

4.2. The Consumer agrees to use the reservation document only for lawful purposes and in line with its nature (as a flight reservation), and not to alter it.

4.3. The Company undertakes to create the reservation in line with the order and to deliver the document within a reasonable time (usually 30 minutes) of payment confirmation.

4.4. The Company does not guarantee the outcome of the visa application. Visa decisions rest solely with the competent authorities; a visa refusal does not entitle the Consumer to a refund of the service fee.

4.5. Flight cancellations, schedule changes or system changes made by the airline are beyond the Company’s control. In such cases the Company will create a new reservation free of charge if requested within the validity period.
