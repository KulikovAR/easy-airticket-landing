## 2. Main characteristics of the service

**Service:** Creating a flight reservation in an airline reservation system for the route and dates chosen by the consumer, for use in a visa application, and delivering the reservation document (including the PNR code) electronically.

**Important:** A reservation is not a flight ticket and cannot be used for travel, boarding or entry into a country. It is valid for up to 7 days and is then cancelled automatically by the airline. The Company makes no promise regarding the outcome of a visa application; visa decisions rest solely with the competent authorities of the country concerned.
